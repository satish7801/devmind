OverviewThis document describes the phased implementation plan for DevMind v1. The plan is structured into four phases over approximately 20 weeks, starting with foundational infrastructure and core modules, progressing to advanced features, external integrations, and a production-ready launch.Each phase has clear deliverables, success criteria, and dependencies. The plan assumes an agile sprint model (2-week sprints) with continuous deployment to staging.2.
