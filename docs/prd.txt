Executive SummaryDevMind is an AI-powered software development intelligence platform purpose-built for engineering organizations. It unifies code intelligence, sprint analytics, knowledge management, documentation generation, and developer onboarding into a single platform — trained on your organization's own codebase, history, and team patterns.Unlike general-purpose AI coding tools that operate at the individual developer level, DevMind operates at the organizational level — becoming smarter with every PR, sprint, and architectural decision your team makes.2. Problem Statement2.1 Current ChallengesKnowledge silos: critical architectural context lives in individual developers' heads and is lost when they leaveSlow code reviews: teams lack org-aware automated review — generic AI tools don't understand your standards or historyUnpredictable sprints: sprint failures are identified after the fact, not predicted in advanceDocumentation debt: documentation is written manually, falls out of date, and is disconnected from actual codeSlow onboarding: new engineers take 3–6 weeks to become productive due to lack of structured codebase contextArchitecture drift: codebases drift away from architectural decisions with no automated detection2.2 Business ImpactProblemCurrent CostDevMind TargetOnboarding time (to first PR)~18 days avg< 6 days (−67%)PR review cycle time~4.2 hours avg< 2.5 hours (−40%)Prod bugs from merged PRs~12 / sprint< 8 / sprint (−33%)Sprint completion rate~61%> 85% (+24pp)Doc coverage across codebase~38%> 80% (+42pp)3. Goals & Non-Goals3.1 GoalsReduce time-to-productivity for new engineers by 60%+Reduce production bugs caught post-merge by 30%+Increase sprint completion predictability to 85%+Achieve 80%+ codebase documentation coverageEliminate knowledge loss when team members transition off a moduleProvide a single intelligence platform usable by devs, tech leads, PMs, and engineering managers3.2 Non-Goals (v1)DevMind is not an IDE plugin or code autocomplete tool — it operates at the org/team levelDevMind does not replace GitHub, Jira, or Slack — it integrates with themDevMind does not train on code beyond the organization's own repositoriesMobile-native app is not in scope for v14.
